# ADR for Dummies: 0010 - Unified Package Checkout & Cart Engine

* **Status:** Approved
* **Date:** 2026-09-13
* **Target Audience:** Non-technical stakeholders, Product Managers, Finance

---

## 1. What problem are we solving?
Previously, travelers had to pay for Visa applications, agency services (like private security or drivers), and professional bookings separately.

We are updating the payment engine so travelers can add multiple items to a cart and pay for everything in one single transaction.

## 2. Key Benefits
* **Single Checkout:** Travelers pay once for their entire trip package.
* **Unified Invoice:** Itemized receipts showing exact breakdowns of visa fees, agency costs, and professional rates.
